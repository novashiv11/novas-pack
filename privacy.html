Privacy notice

Nova's Pack is a free community for people who love animals. We keep as little about you as we can, and we never sell it. Here's exactly what happens to your information.

Who's responsible

Nova's Pack is run by Shiv (Nova's human), an individual based in Ireland, who decides how your information is used (the "controller").

Contact: Instagram DM to @novathehumanhandler, or, once it opens, a moderator in the pack chat on Discord.

We don't collect your address, date of birth, or anything about your health. Please don't send us those.

Where it's kept

In Nova's Instagram account, on the Discord server, and in a short private note on Shiv's phone (which may be included in the phone's cloud backup). Nothing is sent to or kept on this website. What you write in the pet care diary stays on your own device (see below).

Who else sees it

  • Meta: Instagram is provided in the EU by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd, as its own controller, under its own privacy policy. It shares some data with Meta companies in the US under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Discord: the pack chat runs on Discord, which processes your account and messages as its own controller, under its own privacy policy, and may transfer data to the US.
  • Our volunteer moderators see what they need to approve members and keep the chat safe.
  • Other pack members see what you post in the chat.
  • Everyone on Instagram sees a Shout-out Saturday feature or a reshare, but only if you said yes.

We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Members list and our messages: deleted within 30 days of you leaving or asking. Once a year we clear people who never joined the chat.
  • Shout-out consent: kept while the post is up, plus 12 months.
  • If you're removed for breaking a rule: we keep a one-line note (username, date, rule) for 12 months.

Your copy of our messages stays in your own Instagram or Discord, and you can delete it there. A Shout-out post stays up unless you ask us to remove it.

Ages

Following Nova, challenges, Shout-out Saturday and the Pack Card maker are for people aged 16 and over (16 is Ireland's age of digital consent). The pack chat on Discord is for adults, 18 and over. If you're under 16, please don't send us your details; if you do, we'll delete them.

The pet care diary (the app)

The app on our home page keeps your pet's routine: your pets' names, species, birthdays and photos, poops, meals, treats, walks, medicines and doses, vaccines and preventives, weight, food, vet notes and training progress. "Level up together" adds training sessions and skill self-checks, Adventure Book stamps (with an optional photo and note), Academy lessons you've finished, your daily "1% today" ticks, your Growth Journal lines, and the name you'd like Nova to call you, if you give one. Levels, XP and badges are worked out from these on your device; they aren't stored or sent anywhere.

  • Only on your device. All of it is saved in your browser's own storage on that phone or computer (a database called IndexedDB). None of it is ever sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see it. There's no account and no sign-in.
  • Photos stay on your device. A pet or adventure photo is shrunk and re-saved on your device, which removes its location and other hidden details, and it's kept only in that same on-device storage. If you pick "Cartoon", the cartoon is drawn on your device too; the photo is never uploaded.
  • Your journal is private. Growth Journal lines are kept only on your device, like everything else, and are included in your backup file so you don't lose them.
  • Backup and restore. "Export backup" (under More) saves one file to your device. Where you keep it is your choice; if you put it in your email or a cloud drive, that provider holds it under its own terms. "Import backup" reads a backup file on your device; nothing is uploaded.
  • Calendar reminders. "Add to calendar" makes a small calendar file on your device. If you add it to a calendar app, that reminder is then stored by your calendar provider.
  • Delete it any time. Use "Delete all data" under More, delete a single pet, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Deleting is permanent: we have no copy, so we can't restore it.
  • Keeping it safe. The app asks your browser to keep its storage (so it isn't cleared to free up space) and stores the app's own files for offline use. Some browsers, such as Safari, may clear a website's data if it isn't used for a while, so installing the app on your home screen and exporting a backup now and then are a good idea.
  • Not a vet. The diary helps you keep notes. It doesn't diagnose or suggest doses. If you're worried about your pet, call your vet.

Because this information never leaves your device, we don't collect or process it, and there's nothing for us to share, sell or hand over.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and nothing loaded from other sites (even the fonts are stored here).

  • Pack Card maker: your photo is processed in your own browser and never uploaded. Making the card removes the photo's location data.
  • Saved on your device: your card choices (not the photo) and your Pack streak are kept in your browser's local storage, only on that device. Delete them with the button on the Pack Card page, or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Pet care diary: see the pet care diary above. No data from it ever leaves your device.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as its own controller; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Your pet care diary is fully in your hands: you can see, change, export and delete it in the app. For anything we do keep (above), you can ask to see, correct or delete your information; to limit how we use it; to get a copy to take elsewhere; to object; and to withdraw consent at any time (it won't affect what we did before). Just message us; we'll reply within a month.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell the pack chat.
