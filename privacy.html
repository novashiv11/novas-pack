Privacy notice

Nova's Pack is a free community for people who love animals. We keep as little about you as we can, and we never sell it. Here's exactly what happens to your information.

Who's responsible

Nova's Pack is run by Shiv (Nova's human), an individual based in Limerick, Ireland, who decides how your information is used (the "controller").

Contact: Instagram DM to @novathehumanhandler, or ask any admin in the WhatsApp pack chat to pass your request on.

What we keep, and why

WhatWhyLegal basis
Your name or Instagram handle, and your animals' names and species (or "no pet yet"), from your PACK messageTo welcome you and add you to the pack chatLegitimate interest in running a free community you asked to join. You can object at any time.
Your phone number and WhatsApp name (WhatsApp shows these to admins)To approve you into the chat and keep it safeLegitimate interest (as above)
Your rough area, e.g. "Castletroy" (only if you give it)To tell you about walks near youYour consent
Your animal's photo and your handle, for Shout-out SaturdayTo feature your animal on Nova's InstagramYour consent, asked each time
For Pack Walks (when they start): your first name, your dog's name, and that you accepted the walk rulesTo run a safe, small walkLegitimate interest
Notes on rule-breaking (first name, date, what happened)To keep the pack safe and fairLegitimate interest

We don't collect your address, date of birth, or anything about your health. Please don't send us those.

Where it's kept

In Shiv's Instagram and WhatsApp accounts, and a short members list in a private note on Shiv's phone. Nothing is kept on this website.

Who else sees it

  • Meta (Instagram and WhatsApp) processes your messages as its own controller, under its own privacy policies. Meta may transfer data to the US under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Other pack members see what you post in the chat, and may see your phone number.
  • The walk trainer sees walkers' first names and dogs' names.
  • Everyone on Instagram sees a Shout-out Saturday feature, but only if you said yes.

We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you leave the pack or ask us to delete it. Our notes are deleted within 30 days of that. Your copy of our messages stays in your own Instagram or WhatsApp, and you can delete it there. A Shout-out post stays up unless you ask us to remove it.

Ages

Following Nova, photo challenges and Shout-out Saturday are for people aged 16 and over (16 is Ireland's age of digital consent). The WhatsApp pack chat and Pack Walks are for adults, 18 and over. If you're under 16, please don't send us your details; if you do, we'll delete them.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and nothing loaded from other sites: even the fonts are stored here. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as its own controller; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information; to limit how we use it; to get a copy to take elsewhere; to object; and to withdraw consent at any time (it won't affect what we did before). Just message us; we'll reply within a month.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell the pack chat.
