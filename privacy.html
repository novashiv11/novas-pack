Privacy notice

Nova's Pack is a free community for people who love animals. We keep as little about you as we can, and we never sell it. Here's exactly what happens to your information.

Who's responsible

Nova's Pack is run by Shiv (Nova's human), an individual based in Ireland, who decides how your information is used (the "controller").

Contact: Instagram DM to @novathehumanhandler, or message a moderator in the pack chat on Discord.

What we keep, and why

WhatWhyLegal basis
Your name or Instagram handle, and your animals' names and species (or "no pet yet"), if you message usTo welcome you and send you the pack chat linkLegitimate interest in running a free community you asked to join. You can object at any time.
Your Discord username, your answers to the join questions (who's in your family; that you're 18+ and accept the rules) and your postsTo run the pack chat, give badges and keep it safeLegitimate interest (as above)

Notes on rule-breaking (username, date, which rule)To keep the pack safe and fairLegitimate interest

We don't collect your address, date of birth, or anything about your health. Please don't send us those.

Where it's kept

In Nova's Instagram account, on the Discord server, and in a short private note on Shiv's phone (which may be included in the phone's cloud backup). Nothing is sent to or kept on this website.

Who else sees it

  • Meta: Instagram is provided in the EU by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd, as its own controller, under its own privacy policy. It shares some data with Meta companies in the US under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Discord: the pack chat runs on Discord, which processes your account and messages as its own controller, under its own privacy policy, and may transfer data to the US.
  • Our volunteer moderators see what they need to approve members and keep the chat safe.
  • Other pack members see what you post in the chat.
  • Everyone on Instagram sees a Shout-out Saturday feature or a reshare, but only if you said yes.

We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Members list and our messages: deleted within 30 days of you leaving or asking. Once a year we clear people who never joined the chat.
  • Shout-out consent: kept while the post is up, plus 12 months.
  • If you're removed for breaking a rule: we keep a one-line note (username, date, rule) for 12 months.

Your copy of our messages stays in your own Instagram or Discord, and you can delete it there. A Shout-out post stays up unless you ask us to remove it.

Ages

Following Nova, challenges, Shout-out Saturday and the Pack Card maker are for people aged 16 and over (16 is Ireland's age of digital consent). The pack chat on Discord is for adults, 18 and over. If you're under 16, please don't send us your details; if you do, we'll delete them.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and nothing loaded from other sites (even the fonts are stored here).

  • Pack Card maker: your photo is processed in your own browser and never uploaded. Making the card removes the photo's location data.
  • Saved on your device: your card choices (not the photo) and your Pack streak are kept in your browser's local storage, only on that device. Delete them with the button on the Pack Card page, or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as its own controller; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information; to limit how we use it; to get a copy to take elsewhere; to object; and to withdraw consent at any time (it won't affect what we did before). Just message us; we'll reply within a month.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell the pack chat.
