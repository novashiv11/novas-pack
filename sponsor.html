What it does for your clients

  • 💉

    Remembers the dates

    Vaccines, preventives and medicines get due dates and calendar reminders.

  • 📋

    Arrives prepared

    A one-page vet summary: contacts, food, medicines, vaccines, weight and recent notes.

  • 🩺

    Knows when to call

    The Unwell checklist points owners to you. It never diagnoses and never gives doses.

  • 🐾

    Practises calm handling

    Short, reward-based steps for paws, ears and carriers before the visit.
