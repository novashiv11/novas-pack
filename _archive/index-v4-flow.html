Good to know

💛 House rules
  • No silly questions, no judgement. Any species, any experience.
  • No selling or rehoming animals, even for free. A registered rescue will help.
  • Vet first. Worried your animal is unwell? Call your vet. We never diagnose.
  • Kind methods only. No hitting, shock, prong or choke chains.
  • Keep it private. No addresses, live locations or other people's details.
  • No uninvited DMs. Be kind, or be gone.
🆘 Lost or found a pet?
  1. Call local shelters, vets and animal control (in Ireland, your council's dog warden).
  2. Check the microchip: a vet can scan a found pet. In Europe, Europetnet shows which database holds a chip.
  3. Post on local lost and found pages (in Ireland, the ISPCA register).
  4. Tag @novathehumanhandler or post in the pack chat and we'll help share.

Never pay a "finder" before you've seen your pet. Animal in danger? Contact your local welfare charity or police (Ireland: ISPCA 0818 515 515).
