# What we take from Duolingo (CRQ-027)

Duolingo turned a hard, boring habit into something people open every day. We copy what makes it **easy and fun**, and leave out what makes it **anxious** (streak-loss fear, guilt notifications, paywalled hearts).

| Duolingo does | Why it works | Nova's Pack version |
|---|---|---|
| **One glowing next step:** the home screen is a path with one "Start" node ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design)) | No choice paralysis | Home leads with one big **Do 1 minute now** button. Everything else is compact or one tap away |
| **One question per screen,** a progress bar on top, a big button at the bottom | Low effort per tap; you always know how far is left | The **level check** and lesson quizzes are one card per screen with a progress bar, and the answer is a big tappable picture card |
| **Instant feedback and celebration:** sounds, Duo's animations, a "lesson complete" screen with XP counting up ([60fps design](https://60fpsdesign.substack.com/p/fun-in-every-frame)) | The reward lands right after the effort | A **Mission complete** screen after every session: Nova bounces, XP counts up, today's week dot fills, a sticker drops in. Calm with reduced motion |
| **Streak plus streak freeze:** "the fear of losing a streak can stop people starting" ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)) | Habit through repetition in the same context | We keep **3 days wins the week** and grace weeks. No loss mechanics, no countdowns |
| **Pick a daily goal and a reminder time** in onboarding | An implementation intention, set once | **"When should Nova nudge you?"** is one tap (morning, lunch, after work, evening), then a calendar reminder. This replaces the confusing "plan" |
| **Mascot with personality** in every moment | An emotional connection | Nova reacts with short lines, never paragraphs |
| **Tips live in the "guidebook",** not on the lesson screen | Screens stay light | Tips are behind **"How?"**. Home has no paragraphs; extras sit in a small row of icon tiles |
| **Placement test** at the start, game-like | Respect for what you already know | Our level check: six one-tap cards, with a result screen and your first 1-minute win |
| **Shareable milestones** | Pride and free growth | The **trophy card** (Story size) after a level-up, a week won or a badge (CRQ-042) |

**We don't copy:**
- leaderboards (they need a server and invite comparison);
- hearts or lives (they punish mistakes);
- guilt-trip notifications ("these reminders don't seem to be working");
- paywalls on progress.

## Changes this sprint
1. Home: one mission card first, one-tap logs, a compact partner card, and an icon row for extras (art, journal, badges, stickers, academy).
2. Level check: one question per screen with a progress bar and big cards, a result screen, then straight into the first win.
3. Mission complete screen after each session.
4. "When should Nova nudge you?" replaces "Make a 30-second plan".
5. Less text everywhere: one line of copy per card at most; tips behind "How?".
