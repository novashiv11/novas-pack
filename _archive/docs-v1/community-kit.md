## 2. Pinned messages

### House rules (pin in every group)

> **🐾 House rules (each one has a reason)**
> 1. **No silly questions.** Everyone started somewhere. Help, don't lecture.
> 2. **No judgement.** Any species, any breed, any experience.
> 3. **No selling, giving away, swapping or rehoming animals here, even for free.** No breeding or stud offers. (Irish law has strict rules on advertising pets, and "free to a good home" posts attract the wrong people.) Need to rehome? A registered rescue will help.
> 4. **Vet first.** If your animal seems unwell, call your vet before you post. We share experience; nobody here diagnoses.
> 5. **Keep it private.** No home addresses, exact spots or live locations. Don't post anyone else's details or health info.
> 6. **No uninvited DMs.** Don't message members privately unless they've said yes. Report it and they're removed.
> 7. **Your photos, your animals.** Share photos you took, of animals you look after.
> 8. **No ads.** Businesses: talk to an admin first. Nobody can pay for recommendations.
> 9. **Be kind, or be gone.** No shaming, pile-ons or spam.
>
> 18+ only. Privacy: novashiv11.github.io/novas-pack/privacy.html

### Emergency pin (pin in The Pack)

> **🚨 Emergencies: don't wait for the chat**
> - **Your animal is hurt or very unwell:** ring your vet now. Out of hours, your vet's answering message gives the emergency number.
> - **A person is in danger:** 999 or 112.
> - **An animal is being hurt or neglected:** ISPCA cruelty line **0818 515 515** (Mon–Fri, 9–5) or ispca.ie/report-cruelty. Out of hours: the Gardaí.
> - **A stray or dangerous dog in Limerick:** Limerick dog shelter, **061 301604**.
> - **Lost or found pet:** Limerick Animal Lost & Found (Facebook), the ISPCA register, then share it here.
>
> Admins check the chat twice a day. We're volunteers, not an emergency service. Please don't post cruelty images or anyone's address: report them instead.

## 3. Moderation playbook

### The ladder

| Step | When | What the admin does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First minor slip (off-topic, rude, an ad) | Delete the post, DM a friendly reminder of the rule. |
| 2 | Second time | Final warning by DM: "Next time we'll remove you." |
| 3 | Third time | Remove from the community. |
| **Straight out** | Selling or rehoming animals, cruelty, harassment, uninvited DMs (reported), threats, hate, scams, adult content, sharing someone's location, anyone clearly under 18 | Delete, remove, no warning. If there's a risk to a person, ring the Gardaí. |

**Log:** a private note with three columns: first name · date · what happened. Delete a line 30 days after the person leaves.

### Common situations

| Situation | Reply / action |
|---|---|
| A health question with worrying signs | "Please ring your vet now, and tell us how you got on." Don't let the thread guess. |
| A cruelty or neglect report | Thank them; point to the emergency pin; remove any photo or address of the alleged owner. |
| Someone shares their address or a live location | Ask them to delete it; delete it yourself after an hour. |
| A "free to good home" or "rehoming fee" post | Delete; DM the rehoming reply (section 9). |
| A pet business advertising | Delete; DM: "Thanks! We don't allow ads, but members can recommend places they've used for the map." |

### Scam warnings (post in Announcements once a month)

> **🛡️ Scam watch.** Lost-pet and pet-sale scams target groups like ours.
> - **Never share a 6-digit code** someone texts you, even if they say it's to "verify you're the owner". It's your WhatsApp being stolen.
> - **Never pay a "finder"** or a deposit before you've seen the animal, in person, in a public place.
> - In lost-pet posts, **leave out one identifying mark**, so you can test anyone who says they've found your pet.

## 4. Meetup safety rules (all in-person meetups)

1. **Public, on-lead places only.** Never someone's home or garden. Not off-lead enclosures (e.g. Mungret dog park, where treats are also banned).
2. **Leads on the whole time:** a fixed lead of 2 m or less, no retractable leads. **One dog per adult.**
3. **Adults only (18+).**
4. **The law, for every dog:** licensed, microchipped, collar with the owner's name and address. **Restricted breeds** (German Shepherds like Nova included, and crosses): muzzled and on a strong lead of 2 m or less, held by an adult who can control them. **XL Bully types:** bring your Certificate of Exemption. ([gov.ie](https://www.gov.ie/en/publication/905aa-my-legal-responsibilities-as-a-dog-owner/))
5. **You're responsible for your own dog at all times** (owners are strictly liable for their dogs under the Control of Dogs Act). The trainer or organiser may ask any dog to sit out.
6. **No dog-to-dog greetings.** Keep **5 m** between dogs; the trainer says when it's OK to close up.
7. **Not for dogs who find other dogs hard.** Plenty of great dogs need more space than a group gives. They skip group walks; the trainer will suggest calmer options, including at least one that isn't their own paid service.
8. **Healthy, vaccinated, not in season.** Puppies once your vet says they're fully vaccinated.
9. **If a dog gets worried:** the handler turns away and adds distance. If it happens again, they leave calmly. No blame, ever.
10. **Weather:** cancelled for a Met Éireann orange or red warning, posted at least 3 hours before.
11. **Photos:** ask before photographing people or their dogs. Never post the meeting point while the walk is on.

## 5. Pack Walk: format and sign-up

### Before the first walk (must be done)

- [ ] Trainer signed, with **their insurance certificate naming group walks**, and a named backup trainer.
- [ ] **Public liability insurance for you as organiser** (through a broker; check any cap on the number of dogs).
- [ ] **Council check:** ask Limerick City and County Council whether an organised walk in your chosen park needs permission.
- [ ] Route walked once by you: width, traffic, bins, how busy it is with other dogs. Pick an on-lead park (e.g. People's Park: confirm the current by-laws with the council).
- [ ] The sign-up includes the **rules tick-box** (below). It's an acknowledgement of owners' duties, **not** a waiver of liability (those are unfair terms in Ireland).

### Format

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Same day, time and place every week: [Saturday/Sunday] at [10:00]. 45 minutes. |
| **Size** | Max **6 dogs** for the first 4 walks, then up to **8**. Allow 8 sign-ups for 6 places (free events get a lot of no-shows), with a waitlist. |
| **Roles** | The **trainer leads**. The **co-admin** checks people in and walks at the back. Someone else walks Nova, so the organiser's hands are free. |
| **Sign-up** | **Luma** (free; see `docs/money-plan.md`): capacity cap, waitlist, the rules tick-box, automatic reminders. A WhatsApp Event in Pack Walks Limerick also works, but it can't cap numbers and always allows a "+1", so say "1 sign-up = 1 adult + 1 dog". |

**Running order**

1. **Arrive (5 min).** Dogs wait by cars or well apart. Check-in: "Licence and chip OK? Anything we should know about your dog today?"
2. **Walk (30 min).** One or two lines, 5 m apart. The trainer swaps the order now and then.
3. **One skill (5 min).** e.g. "check-ins": rewarding your dog for looking at you.
4. **Wrap up (5 min).** Next week's date. People leave one at a time.

**Kit:** sign-up list, first-aid kit (human and dog), the nearest vet's number and hours, poo bags, water.

### Sign-up tick-box text (Luma registration question)

> ☐ I'm 18 or over. My dog is licensed, microchipped and wears a collar with my name and address. If my dog is a restricted breed, it will be muzzled and on a lead of 2 m or less. My dog is healthy, vaccinated, not in season, and comfortable around other dogs at a distance. I understand I'm responsible for my dog at all times, and that the trainer may ask us to sit out. I've read the walk rules.

### Sign-up post (Pack Walks Limerick + Announcements)

> 🦮 **Pack Walk #[number]: [Day] [date], [time]**
> A calm, on-lead, parallel walk, led by [trainer name] from [business]. 45 minutes. **Max 6 dogs.** Adults only.
> Sign up here: [Luma link]. First 6 are in, then there's a waitlist.
> Can't make it? Cancel on the link so someone else gets your spot.
> Not a group-walk dog? No problem at all: ask [trainer] about calmer options. 💛

### Day-before post

> 📍 **Tomorrow's Pack Walk:** [time] at [public landmark]. Look for a German Shepherd in a muzzle being extremely dignified about it. Running late? Post here. Cancelled for orange or red weather warnings, posted by [time].

### After the walk (same day)

> Thank you, walkers! 🐾 [One line about how it went.] Same time next week: [Luma link].

Post one photo only if everyone in it said yes. Send a personal "see you next week?" to last week's walkers.

## 7. Trainer partnership

### Who to approach

Must have all of these:
- Reward-based only: no shock, prong or choke collars, used or sold.
- A member of a body with a no-aversive code of practice (for example APDT, IMDT, or the ABTC register; check their directory listing).
- An insurance certificate that names group walks.
- Commits to 8 weeks in a row, with a named backup.
- Agrees to disclose any paid offer they make to members, and to suggest at least one calmer option for reactive dogs that isn't their own paid service.

### First message (DM or email)

> Hi [name], I'm Shiv, and I live in Limerick with Nova, a one-year-old German Shepherd (@novathehumanhandler, about 3,800 followers). I'm starting **Nova's Pack**, a free, friendly community for local animal lovers, and I'd love a reward-based trainer to co-run a weekly **Pack Walk**: a calm, on-lead parallel walk for up to 6 dogs, 45 minutes, in a public park.
>
> What's in it for you:
> - You're named as the walk's trainer in every post and on our website.
> - Small paid workshops together, with a split we agree that works for you.
> - Honest promotion: we never sell rankings, and members recommend people by name.
>
> What we'd ask: about an hour a week for 8 weeks, your insurance certificate covering group walks, and a quick check of our short "Learn with Nova" clips before they go up.
>
> Could we have a 15-minute chat, or a trial walk with Nova and a couple of friends' dogs? Thanks so much,
> Shiv (and Nova, who has approved you in principle)

**Questions for the call:** methods and memberships; insurance (group walks? max dogs?); group size they're comfortable with; what they'd teach in 5 minutes; how they'd handle a dog that reacts; 8-week availability and backup; workshop topics, prices and split.

## 10. Weekly rhythm (about 5 hours)

| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Daily, 08:00 and 20:00 | Instagram Requests + WhatsApp approvals; welcome newcomers by name. | ~10 min a day |
| Sunday | Film a batch of Reels and Stories for the week. Count weekly active members (see `docs/launch-kit.md`). | 90 min |
| Monday | Announcements: "This week in the pack" (challenge, walk). | 10 min |
| Wednesday | A question in The Pack (e.g. "What face does your pet make when you open the fridge?"). | 10 min |
| Friday | Pick Shout-out Saturday and ask for consent (screenshot each "yes"). | 30 min |
| Saturday | Post Shout-out Saturday. | 15 min |
| Weekend | Pack Walk (once live). | 1.5 h |
| Flexible | Outreach: trainer, flyers, local groups. | 60 min |

**Never skip a week.** A small, reliable rhythm beats a big, patchy one.
