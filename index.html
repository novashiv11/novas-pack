What the rules mean
  • No selling or rehoming: no selling, giving away or rehoming animals here, even for free. No breeding or stud offers. A registered rescue will help.
  • Vet first: worried your animal is unwell? Call your vet before you post. We share experience; we never diagnose.
  • Kind methods: no advice to hit, shock, prong or choke-chain an animal.
  • Private: no home addresses, live locations or anyone else's details.
  • Be kind: no shaming, pile-ons or spam. Mods remove posts that break the rules, and repeat offenders.

🆘 Lost or found a pet?
  1. Call local shelters, vets and animal control (in Ireland, your council's dog warden).
  2. Check the microchip: a vet can scan a found pet. In Europe, Europetnet shows which database holds a chip.
  3. Post on local lost and found pages (in Ireland, the ISPCA register).
  4. Tag @novathehumanhandler or post in the pack chat and we'll help share.

Never pay a "finder" before you've seen your pet. Animal in danger? Contact your local welfare charity or police (Ireland: ISPCA 0818 515 515).
