The Pack Walk

Coming soon

A calm weekly walk for dogs and their people in Limerick. Everyone stays on lead, side by side with plenty of space between dogs (a "parallel walk"), so dogs get used to each other without the chaos of a meet-and-greet. Co-run with a reward-based trainer, who'll share one small thing to practise. Adults (18+). Sign-ups open in the chat once our trainer is confirmed.

Walk rules
  • Public, on-lead places only. The meeting point is shared in the chat.
  • Leads on the whole time: a fixed lead of 2 m or less, no retractable leads. One dog per adult.
  • No dog-to-dog greetings. That's the whole point of a parallel walk.
  • All dogs: licensed, microchipped, and wearing a collar with your name and address.
  • German Shepherds (Nova included) and other restricted breeds wear a muzzle and a strong lead of 2 m or less, held by an adult who can control them. It's Irish law, and Nova wears hers with dignity. XL Bully types: bring your Certificate of Exemption.
  • Plenty of great dogs need more space than a group walk gives. They skip this one; their people are very welcome, and we'll point you to calmer options.
  • Healthy, up to date with vaccinations, and not in season. Puppies once your vet says they're fully vaccinated.
  • You're responsible for your own dog at all times. The trainer may ask any dog to sit out.
  • Poo bags and water, always. We cancel for Met Éireann orange or red warnings.
  • Ask before photographing other people or their dogs.

House rules

Kind by default

  • No silly questionsEveryone started somewhere. Newbies get help, not lectures.
  • No judgementBig dogs, small hens, old cats, rescue chaos. All welcome.
  • No selling or rehoming animalsNo selling, giving away, swapping or rehoming animals here, even for free. No breeding or stud offers. Need to rehome? A registered rescue will help.
  • Vet firstWorried your animal is unwell? Call your vet before you post. We share experience; we never diagnose.
  • Keep it privateNo home addresses or exact spots ("near Castletroy" is plenty), and no one else's details or health information.
  • No uninvited DMsDon't message members privately unless they've said yes. Report it and they're removed.
  • Be kind, or be goneNo shaming, pile-ons or spam. Admins remove posts, and people who break the rules.

Lost or found a pet?

Any species. Move fast, and go where Limerick already looks:

  1. Post on Limerick Animal Lost & Found (Facebook, run by volunteers).
  2. Report it on the ISPCA lost and found register.
  3. Check the microchip details. Europetnet shows which Irish database holds a chip number. Ring local vets and the Limerick dog shelter (061 301604).
  4. Then share that post in the pack chat so we can spread the word.

Found a dog? A vet can scan for a chip. By law you return it to its owner, hand it to the dog warden, or keep it and tell the warden or Gardaí in writing.

Tip from the ISPCA: don't mention a reward on posters, and never pay a "finder" before you've seen your pet. See an animal in danger? ISPCA cruelty line 0818 515 515 (Mon–Fri, 9–5) or report online; out of hours, the Gardaí.
