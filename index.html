The Pack Walk

A calm, weekly group walk for dogs and their people. We walk on lead, side by side with plenty of space between dogs: a "parallel walk". Dogs get used to each other without the chaos of a free-for-all greeting, and people get a chat. Each walk is co-run with a reward-based trainer, who'll share one small thing to practise along the way.

Walk rules

  • Public places only. The meeting point is shared in the pack chat.
  • Leads on, the whole time.
  • Adults only (18+).
  • Dogs who find other dogs stressful (reactive dogs) sit out group walks. No judgement at all: ask the trainer about calmer options.
  • Restricted breeds, German Shepherds like Nova included, wear a muzzle and a strong lead no longer than 2 m, as Irish law requires.
  • Healthy, vaccinated and not in season. Not sure? Check with your vet first.
  • Bring poo bags and water. Leave every place cleaner than we found it.

Learn with Nova

Short clips of Nova and her human working on the basics: coming back when called, walking on a loose lead, and settling down. First up: recall, loose lead and settle.

This is our journey, not expert instruction. Every clip is checked by a reward-based trainer before it goes up. Kind methods only: treats, toys and praise. No shock, prong or choke collars, ever.

If your animal has a behaviour or health worry, a qualified trainer or your vet beats any clip on the internet, ours included.

House rules

Kind by default

  • No silly questionsEveryone started somewhere. Newbies get help, not lectures.
  • No judgementBig dogs, small hens, old cats, rescue chaos. All welcome.
  • No selling animalsNo buying, selling or rehoming animals for money. This is a community, not a marketplace.
  • Vet firstAny health question gets "check with your vet" first. We share experience; we never diagnose.
  • Keep locations vagueNever share your home address or exact spot. "Near Castletroy" is plenty.
  • Be kind, or be goneNo shaming, pile-ons or spam. Admins remove posts, and people, that break the rules.

Lost or found a pet?

Move fast, and go where Limerick already looks:

  1. Post on Limerick Animal Lost & Found (Facebook, run by volunteers).
  2. Report it on the ISPCA lost and found register.
  3. Check or update the microchip details. Europetnet shows which Irish database holds a chip number. Tell local vets and the dog warden too.
  4. Then share that post in the pack chat so we can spread the word.

Tip from the ISPCA: don't mention a reward on posters. It can attract the wrong kind of attention.

Ages 16+ and your privacy

16+Nova's Pack is for people aged 16 and over. Pack Walks are for adults (18+).

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. Our fonts are stored here, not loaded from Google.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

When you join

  • What we keep: your name or Instagram handle, your animals' names and species (or "no pet yet"), and, only if you want walk news, a rough area like "Castletroy". Never your address.
  • Why: to welcome you, add you to the pack chat, and, only with your OK each time, feature your animal on Shout-out Saturday.
  • Legal basis: your consent. You can withdraw it any time.
  • How long: until you leave the pack or ask us to delete it. We never sell or share it.
  • Apps we use: Instagram and WhatsApp (both Meta) handle your messages under their own privacy policies. In WhatsApp groups, other members may be able to see your phone number.
  • Your rights: see, correct or delete your data, or object. Just DM us. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission.
  • Who we are: Nova's Pack is run by Shiv, Nova's human, in Limerick, Ireland. Contact: DM @novathehumanhandler.

Photos: please share only your own animals. Screenshots and many apps strip location data, but check before posting, and never show where you live. A full privacy policy is coming soon.
