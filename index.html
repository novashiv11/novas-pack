House rules

Kind by default

  • No silly questionsEveryone started somewhere. Newbies get help, not lectures.
  • No judgementAny species, any breed, any experience. All welcome.
  • No selling or rehoming animalsNo selling, giving away or rehoming animals here, even for free. No breeding or stud offers. A registered rescue will help.
  • Vet firstWorried your animal is unwell? Call your vet before you post. We share experience; we never diagnose.
  • Kind methods onlyNo advice to hit, shock, prong or choke-chain an animal.
  • Keep it privateNo home addresses, live locations, or anyone else's details.
  • No uninvited DMsAsk first. Report anyone who doesn't, and they're removed.
  • Be kind, or be goneNo shaming, pile-ons or spam.

Lost or found a pet?

Act fast, and go local first. We're an online pack, so we can't run searches, but we'll share your post.

  1. Call local animal shelters, vets and animal control (in Ireland, your council's dog warden).
  2. Check the microchip: a vet or shelter can scan a found pet. Owners: make sure your details are up to date. In Europe, Europetnet shows which database holds a chip.
  3. Post on your area's lost and found pages (in Ireland, the ISPCA register).
  4. Then share that post in the pack chat so we can spread the word.

Never pay a "finder" before you've seen your pet in person. See an animal in danger? Contact your local animal welfare charity or the police (in Ireland: ISPCA 0818 515 515).
