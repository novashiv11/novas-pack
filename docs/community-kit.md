## 1. Set up the WhatsApp Community (once, ~30 minutes)

### Structure

Keep it small. Four spaces to start; add a species group only when 10+ members ask for it.

| Space | Who can post | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **📣 Nova's Pack: Announcements** (built in) | Admins only | Weekly plan, walk dates, Shout-out Saturday, new challenges. Max 3 posts a week. |
| **🐾 The Pack** | Everyone | Hellos, photos, chat, tips. Any species. |
| **❓ Ask the Pack** | Everyone | Questions, kind answers. Vet-first rule pinned. |
| **🦮 Pack Walks Limerick** | Everyone | Walk sign-ups, meeting point, "running late". Dog-first. |

The vouched map starts as a pinned "send me your favourite places" message in **The Pack**. It gets its own group only when it's live.

### Settings (tap the community name → settings)

- **Community settings → Who can add groups:** Only admins.
- **Each group → Group settings → Approve new members:** On. Approve only people you've had a PACK DM from.
- **Each group → Group settings → Edit group settings:** Only admins.
- **Announcements → Phone number privacy:** On, if your WhatsApp version shows it.
- **Disappearing messages:** Off (rules and walk details need to stay).
- **Invite link:** never post it publicly. Send it only in DMs after someone has said PACK. If it leaks, go to the community → **Invite members** → **Reset link**.

### Community description (paste into the community description)

> Nova's Pack: everyone's welcome in the pack. A free, friendly community for anyone who loves animals, starting in Limerick. Ages 16+. Kind by default: no silly questions, no judgement, no selling animals, vet first on health. Run by Shiv (Nova's human). Privacy: novashiv11.github.io/novas-pack/#privacy

### Admins

- Week 1: Shiv.
- By week 4: ask one helpful, kind regular to co-admin (approve joins, remove spam). Don't do moderation alone past 30 members.

## 3. House rules (full version, for the website and admins)

1. **No silly questions.** Everyone was new once. Answer the question asked; skip "you should have known".
2. **No judgement.** No breed, species, diet, or lifestyle wars. Rescue, bred, adopted, fostered: all welcome.
3. **No selling, buying or rehoming animals for money.** This includes "rehoming fee" posts and studs. We point people to registered rescues instead.
4. **Vet first.** Any health topic gets "check with your vet" first. Nobody diagnoses or suggests medication doses.
5. **Kind methods only.** No advice to use shock, prong or choke collars, or to punish an animal. Ask a reward-based trainer.
6. **Keep locations vague.** No home addresses, exact routes, or live locations. Rough areas only ("near Castletroy").
7. **Your own photos.** Share photos you took, of animals you care for. Don't share other people's photos or faces without asking.
8. **No ads or spam.** Local businesses: ask an admin first. Paid placement isn't for sale.
9. **Lost and found pets** go to Limerick Animal Lost & Found, the ISPCA register and the microchip database first (see section 9), then the chat.
10. **Be kind, or be gone.** No shaming, pile-ons, harassment or hate.

### What admins do (moderation playbook)

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Off-topic, mild rudeness, first time | Private message, friendly reminder of the rule. |
| Second time | Private warning: "Next time we'll remove you." |
| Third time | Remove from the community. |
| Selling/rehoming animals for money, cruelty, threats, harassment, hate, scams, adult content, anyone clearly under 16 | Delete the message and remove the person straight away. No warning. |
| A post describes an animal in danger now | Tell the poster to call the ISPCA cruelty helpline **0818 515 515** (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm) or [report online](https://ispca.ie/report-cruelty); out of hours, the **Gardaí**. For stray or seized dogs: Limerick City and County Council Dog Shelter, **061 301604**. |
| A health question with worrying signs | Reply: "Please ring your vet now. Come back and tell us how you got on." Don't let the thread diagnose. |
| Someone shares their address or a live location | Ask them to delete it; delete it yourself if they don't within the hour. |
| A "lost pet" post asks for money, or a "finder" asks for a reward upfront | Warn the owner privately: it's a common scam. Don't pay before seeing the animal in person, in a public place. |

Keep a simple note in your phone of warnings given (first name + date). Delete it when someone leaves.

## 4. Meetup safety rules (all in-person meetups)

1. **Public places only.** Never someone's home or garden.
2. **Leads on, the whole time.** Fixed leads (no retractable/flexi leads). Harness or collar that fits.
3. **Adults only (18+).**
4. **Law first.** Every dog wears a collar with the owner's name and address, is microchipped and licensed. Restricted breeds (German Shepherds like Nova included) wear a muzzle and a strong lead no longer than 2 m, held by someone over 16 ([gov.ie](https://www.gov.ie/en/publication/905aa-my-legal-responsibilities-as-a-dog-owner/)).
5. **No reactive dogs in group walks.** If your dog lunges, barks or freezes around other dogs, a group walk isn't the right place yet. Ask the trainer about one-to-one or quieter options. No judgement.
6. **Healthy, vaccinated, not in season.** Puppies only once your vet says they're fully vaccinated.
7. **Space.** Keep at least 2 dog-lengths between dogs. No on-lead greetings unless both owners and the trainer say yes.
8. **If a dog gets worried:** add distance, turn away calmly, and leave if needed. Nobody gets told off for leaving early.
9. **Clean up.** Poo bags on every walk.
10. **Weather and cancellations:** we cancel for storm warnings (Met Éireann orange or red), lightning, or extreme heat. Cancellations go in Announcements by 8am on the day.
11. **Photos:** ask before photographing other people. Never post a walk's meeting point while it's happening.

## 5. Pack Walk format and sign-up post

### Format

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | Weekly, [Saturday or Sunday] at [10:00]. 45 minutes. |
| **Where** | A public park or riverside path in Limerick, wide enough for space between dogs. Meeting point shared in **Pack Walks Limerick** the day before. |
| **Size** | Max **6 dogs** for the first 4 walks; then up to **8** if it's going well. Waitlist beyond that. |
| **Who runs it** | The partner trainer leads. Shiv is the "sweeper" at the back, with the sign-up list and a phone. |
| **Price** | [Free / sponsored]. Paid trainer workshops are separate. |

**Running order**

1. **Arrive (5 min).** Dogs stay in or by cars, or at a distance. The trainer checks names off and reminds everyone of the rules.
2. **Walk in a line (30 min).** Single file or two parallel lines, 2+ dog-lengths apart. The trainer swaps the order now and then so every dog sees others from a calm distance.
3. **One small skill (5 min).** The trainer shows one thing (e.g. "check-ins": rewarding your dog for looking at you). Each person tries it where they stand.
4. **Wrap up (5 min).** Next week's plan. People leave one at a time, with space.

**Kit:** sign-up list, first-aid kit (human and dog), the nearest vet's number and opening hours, poo bags, water.

**Before the first walk (must be done):**
- [ ] Partner trainer confirmed, with their own insurance.
- [ ] Public liability insurance for the walks (ask the trainer if their policy covers group walks; if not, see `docs/ireland-setup-gdpr.md` when written).
- [ ] Route walked once by Shiv: width, traffic, other dogs, bins.
- [ ] Each person ticks the walk rules on sign-up.

### Sign-up post (Pack Walks Limerick and Announcements)

> 🦮 **Pack Walk #[number]: [Day] [date], [time]**
> Calm, on-lead, parallel walk, led by [trainer name] from [business]. 45 minutes. **Max 6 dogs.**
> Meeting point: shared here the day before.
>
> To join, reply with:
> 1. Your first name
> 2. Your dog's name, age and rough size
> 3. "Yes to the walk rules" (pinned above: public place, leads on, adults only, no reactive dogs, muzzle and short lead for restricted breeds, healthy and vaccinated)
>
> First 6 are in; then there's a waitlist. Can't make it? Tell us here so someone else gets your spot.
> Not a group-walk dog? No problem: ask [trainer] about quieter options. 💛

**Pinned in 🦮 Pack Walks Limerick:** the full meetup safety rules (section 4).

### Day-before post

> 📍 **Tomorrow's Pack Walk:** meet at [public landmark, e.g. the car park by X], [time]. Look for the [colour] lead and a German Shepherd in a muzzle being very well behaved about it. Running late? Post here. See you there.

## 7. Trainer partnership pitch

**Who to approach:** reward-based trainers within ~20 minutes of Limerick city, who are insured and belong to a body that requires force-free methods. Check their website and reviews first. Ask about methods directly: walk away from anyone who uses or sells shock, prong or choke collars.

**DM or email:**

> Hi [name], I'm Shiv, and I live in Limerick with Nova, a one-year-old German Shepherd (@novathehumanhandler, about 3,800 followers). I'm starting **Nova's Pack**, a free, friendly community for local animal lovers, and I'd love a reward-based trainer to co-run a weekly **Pack Walk**: a calm, on-lead parallel walk for up to 6 dogs, 45 minutes, in a public park.
>
> What's in it for you:
> - You're named as the walk's trainer in every post and on our website.
> - A natural route to paid one-to-ones and workshops with people who've already met you. [We'd love to run small paid workshops together; happy to agree a split that works for you.]
> - Honest, no-fuss promotion: we never sell rankings, and members vouch for you by name.
>
> What we'd ask: about 1 hour a week, your own insurance, and a quick look at our short "Learn with Nova" training clips before they go up, so we never post anything that isn't kind and correct.
>
> Could we have a 15-minute chat, or a trial walk with Nova and a couple of friends' dogs? Thanks so much,
> Shiv (and Nova, who has already approved you in principle)

**Questions to ask on the call:** methods and qualifications; insurance (does it cover group walks?); max group size they're comfortable with; what they'd teach in 5 minutes per walk; how they'd handle a dog that reacts; availability for 8 weeks; workshop ideas and price.
