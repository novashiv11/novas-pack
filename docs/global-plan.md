# Nova's Pack: the global plan

**Version 3 · 10 Oct 2026 · Replaces the Limerick-first plan.** Online only, worldwide, run from a phone in about 5 hours a week.

## The answer in 6 lines

1. **What:** a free, worldwide online community for everyone who loves animals, hosted by Nova. It's built around a **weekly rhythm** people come back for, not an endless feed.
2. **Where:** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels bring people in (one video, four places). **Discord** is the home (the pack chat). The **website** is the hub, with the **Pack Card maker** as the shareable hook.
3. **How it hooks:** a weekly challenge, Shout-out Saturday, Sunday #PackWalk, a Pack Card for every pet, a weekly streak with grace weeks, and badges for showing up, helping and learning. No infinite scroll, no guilt notifications, no points for likes.
4. **When:** launch in 2 weeks; the first money streams switch on at the 10k-follower and 500-member milestones (section 6).
5. **How much:** year one is roughly **€300 / €1,500 / €4,000** (cautious / base / strong). These are estimates; most of the upside depends on hitting 10k–25k followers.
6. **Founder time:** a 90-minute filming batch on Sundays plus about 30 minutes a day. Everything repetitive is automated or scheduled.

## 1. Why this can work

- **Pet content is one of the most shared things online,** and Instagram's ranking now weighs **sends (shares by DM)** and **watch time** heavily ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-algorithm/), [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works)). Funny, warm pet videos are exactly what people send to friends.
- **Nova has a voice.** The "professional human handler" persona is a character, and characters build audiences that last longer than cute clips alone.
- **Most pet communities are one of two things:** a feed (passive) or a forum (dated). A small, kind community with a *weekly rhythm* and *recognition* is rarer and stickier.
- **Original, real content is rewarded;** aggregators lost reach in 2025 ([eclincher](https://www.eclincher.com/articles/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works-in-2026)). Nova *is* original content.

**Why Discord for the home (not WhatsApp):**

| | Discord | WhatsApp Community | Facebook Group |
|---|---|---|---|
| Members see your phone number | **No** (usernames) | Yes, in groups | No |
| Built-in spam filter and join questions | **Yes** (AutoMod, onboarding) | Approval only | Yes |
| Badges/roles and bots | **Yes** | No | Some badges |
| Scales worldwide | **Yes** | Groups cap around 1,000 | Yes |
| Familiar to 25–55s | Medium | **High** | **High** |

Discord wins on privacy, moderation and gamification. Its weakness is familiarity, so the join steps are written for first-timers. The choice is reversible: the site reads the link from one line in `site.js`.

## 3. How it hooks people (the engagement system)

Built on what makes Instagram, TikTok, Duolingo, Strava and BeReal habit-forming (variable rewards, streaks, recognition, rituals, shareable results). Adapted to the founder's rules: **points only for showing up, helping and learning, never for likes.**

### The weekly rhythm (all times UTC; everyone takes part in their own time zone)

| Day | Ritual | What members do | What they get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | New challenge | Post a photo or video to the theme (on Instagram/TikTok with the tag, or in Discord) | A chance at Shout-out Saturday; a streak tick |
| **Wednesday** | Ask the Pack | Ask or answer in the Q&A forum (vet first on health) | A "Helper" badge for good answers, as voted by members |
| **Saturday** | **Shout-out Saturday** | — | Nova features up to 3 members' animals: a newcomer, the most helpful, and a challenge favourite |
| **Sunday** | **#PackWalk** | Get outside with your pet, or do something real together (garden, window seat, a shelter visit). Post one photo. | A "Walker" stamp; real life over screens |

**Challenge themes (8-week rotation):** #NewToThePack · #OffDuty · #GoldenHour · #SideBySide · #SnackFace · #ThrowbackPet · #MyPetsJob · #NewTrick. The website shows the current one automatically.

### The 8 mechanics we use

| Mechanic | Inspired by | Our version | Where it runs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shareable result** | Wordle, Spotify Wrapped | **Pack Card**: a playing-card-style ID for any pet (or "no pet yet" pack supporter), with a witty title and stats. One tap to share to Stories. | Website (no backend) |
| **Weekly streak with grace** | Duolingo (streak freeze) | Weekly, not daily. 1 grace week a month. Shown on your card. Honour system, saved on your device. | Website |
| **Ritual time** | BeReal | Sunday #PackWalk | Instagram + Discord |
| **Recognition (variable reward)** | Strava kudos, Reddit awards | Shout-out Saturday, chosen by rotation, nomination and Nova's human; **never by likes** | Instagram |
| **Badges for real actions** | Strava challenges, Discord roles | Challenger, Walker, Helper, Learner, Founding 100. Given for doing things, not chatting. | Discord roles |
| **Belonging and identity** | Reddit, fandoms | "The pack", Nova's voice, member titles ("Head of Snack Security") | Everywhere |
| **Remix/UGC loop** | TikTok duets, templates | Monthly CapCut template ("Nova reviews your pet"), challenge hashtags | TikTok/Instagram |
| **Member-voted awards** | Reddit awards | Monthly "Pack Awards" poll in Discord (Most Helpful, Best Nap) | Discord |

### Never (dark patterns we won't use)

- Infinite feeds or autoplay on our own surfaces.
- Guilt notifications ("Nova misses you!") or losing a streak without warning.
- Points, ranks or rewards for likes, follower counts or message volume. (Discord "XP per message" bots stay **off**.)
- Fake counts, fake urgency or fake scarcity.
- Paid rank or paid features in the free community.
- Anything targeting under-16s.

The EU is preparing rules on addictive design and dark patterns (the Digital Fairness Act, expected 2026: [Bird & Bird](https://cm.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/whos-the-fairest-of-them-all-dfa-plans-to-transform-online-consumer-protection), [Sidley](https://datamatters.sidley.com/2025/08/01/eu-consults-on-digital-fairness-act-big-changes-ahead-for-consumer-facing-platforms/)). Staying humane is also staying compliant.

## 4. Growth: how people find the pack

**The engine:** one short video a day on weekdays, posted to all four platforms from one upload.

| # | Tactic | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Nova's inner monologue** | Voiceover in Nova's voice over everyday clips. This is the signature format. |
| 2 | **Built to be sent** | End with "Send this to someone whose cat does this." Sends drive reach. |
| 3 | **Trial Reels** | Test new hooks with non-followers first (Creator account). |
| 4 | **Shout-out Saturday** | Every feature tags members; they reshare; their followers find Nova. |
| 5 | **Pack Card** | Every shared card carries the site link and #NovasPack. |
| 6 | **Weekly challenge hashtag** | Members' posts point back to Nova. |
| 7 | **Collab posts** | Monthly collab Reel with a similar-sized pet account (any species). |
| 8 | **Muzzle-positive** | A recurring series: German Shepherds must wear a muzzle in public in Ireland, and Nova makes it look good. Useful and different. |
| 9 | **Learn with Nova** | Training-journey clips are saved and shared: long-tail reach. |
| 10 | **Reply with video** | Answer a good comment with a video. Cheap content, and people feel seen. |

**Posting times (UTC):** 18:00–19:30 catches Europe's evening and the US morning; add 23:00–01:00 for the US evening once you can (scheduled). Max 5 hashtags; tagging and sends matter more.

**Automation:** film on your phone → edit in CapCut (templates) → **Metricool** free plan or **Meta Business Suite** schedules to Instagram and Facebook; TikTok and YouTube Shorts via Metricool (one channel per network on the free plan; check current limits) or the native schedulers. Upload clean files without another app's watermark.

## 6. How much: money

**Rules:** never pay-to-rank, never sell data, never pet-sale ads, never aversive products. **#Ad** on every paid or gifted post ([CCPC/ASAI](https://www.ccpc.ie/news-and-media/news/article/2023/10/26/if-in-doubt--label-it!---new-guidance-on-social-media-advertising-for-influencers-issued-by-ccpc-and-asai)).

| Stream | Switch on at | How (tool) | Automation | Year 1 (C / B / S) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Affiliate links** (kit Nova actually uses) | Now | Amazon Associates (check which store accepts Irish creators), brand affiliate programmes; link-in-bio page | High: links work by themselves | €50 / €250 / €700 |
| **Brand posts** | 5k+ followers | Media kit + marketplaces (Collabstr, Insense); invoice with Stripe | Medium | €0 / €450 / €1,500 |
| **Sponsored weekly challenge** | 500+ Discord members | A pet brand sponsors a theme week (#Ad, prizes go to members) | Medium | €0 / €200 / €600 |
| **Merch drop** | 500+ members or Christmas | **Fourthwall**: print-on-demand, ships worldwide, handles VAT as seller of record | High | €50 / €250 / €600 |
| **Learn with Nova mini-course** (trainer co-created) | 10k+ followers and 8+ free clips | Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy (seller of record for EU VAT) | High | €0 / €200 / €500 |
| **Clip licensing** (a clip goes viral) | Any time | ViralHog / Newsflare revenue share | High | €0 / €50 / €300 |
| **YouTube Shorts ads** | 1k subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days | YouTube Partner Program | High | €0 / €50 / €200 |
| **Supporter membership** (perks, never paid rank) | 25k followers or 1,000 members | Instagram Subscriptions or Discord Server Subscriptions (check availability in Ireland) | High | €0 / €0 / €300 |
| **Total** | | | | **~€100 / €1,450 / €4,700** |

**Not available or not worth it yet:** TikTok Creator Rewards isn't listed for Ireland (it needs 10k followers, 100k views in 30 days, and videos over 1 minute) ([ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-rewards-program/)). Paid ads: no.

**Costs (year 1):** €0–60. The email and domain are optional; everything else is a free tier.

**Tax, briefly (not advice):**
- Side income under €5,000 net and under €30,000 gross: declare it on Form 12 in Revenue myAccount ([Revenue](https://www.revenue.ie/en/additional-incomes/is-your-extra-income-taxable/index.aspx)).
- Prefer platforms that are the seller of record (Fourthwall, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy), so they handle VAT.
- **Talk to an accountant before the first euro.** Paid tools bought from outside Ireland can trigger VAT registration however small you are (the reverse charge).
- Register "Nova's Pack" as a business name with the CRO (about €20) when you start earning under that name.

## 8. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|
| Growth is slower than hoped | The community still works at 50 people; the weekly rhythm matters more than size. |
| Moderating alone across time zones | AutoMod, join questions, 2 co-mods by week 4, a clear rules ladder. |
| Health questions | Vet-first rule, pinned; AutoMod flags words like "poison", "bleeding", "not breathing" with a vet-first reply. |
| Selling or rehoming animals | Banned outright; AutoMod keywords ("for sale", "rehoming fee", "stud"). |
| Platform dependence | Email list from month 2; the website is ours. |
| Minors | 16+ for Instagram features, 18+ for Discord; join question plus Discord's own age gate. |
| Burnout | One batch day, templates, and a "skip week" allowed once a quarter (announced honestly). |

## Appendix A: Discord setup (from a phone, ~45 minutes)

1. **Create the server:** Discord app → **+** → Create My Own → For a club or community → name "Nova's Pack" → upload Nova's head icon (`img/brand/nova-head.svg` as PNG).
2. **Turn on Community:** Server Settings → Enable Community. This gives rules screening, onboarding, forum channels and announcements.
3. **Channels:**
   - **START HERE:** #welcome-and-rules (read-only), #introduce-your-pack
   - **THIS WEEK:** #announcements (read-only), #weekly-challenge, #pack-walk-sunday, #shout-out-saturday (read-only)
   - **ASK THE PACK:** forum channel with tags (Dogs, Cats, Birds, Small pets, Horses, Behaviour, Training, Health: vet first)
   - **HANG OUT:** #general, #pet-photos
   - **MODS:** #mod-log (private)
4. **Onboarding** (Server Settings → Onboarding):
   - Question 1: "Who's in your family?" Dog / Cat / Bird / Rabbit or small pet / Horse / Reptile or fish / Other / No pet yet. Each gives a role.
   - Question 2: "I'm 18 or over and I accept the house rules." Required.
5. **Rules screening:** paste the house rules (Appendix B). Members must agree before they can post.
6. **AutoMod** (Server Settings → AutoMod):
   - Block spam and mention spam.
   - Block a custom keyword list: "for sale", "rehoming fee", "stud", "puppies available", "kittens available", "cashapp", "deposit".
   - Flag health words to #mod-log: "poison", "bleeding", "not breathing", "seizure", "collapsed".
7. **Roles (badges):** Founding 100 · Challenger · Walker · Helper · Learner · Co-mod. Given by hand on Saturdays (5 min) or via reaction roles later. **No XP-per-message bots.**
8. **Invite link:** Server name → Invite → Edit invite link → Expire after: **Never**, Max uses: **No limit**. Copy it into `site.js` (`COMMUNITY_URL`).
9. **Safety:** Server Settings → Safety Setup → Verification level **Medium** (account older than 5 minutes); DM spam filter on; explicit image filter on for everyone.

## Appendix B: House rules (paste into Discord rules screening)

1. **No silly questions.** Everyone started somewhere. Help, don't lecture.
2. **No judgement.** Any species, any breed, any experience, no pet yet.
3. **No selling, giving away or rehoming animals**, even for free. No breeding or stud offers. Need to rehome? A registered rescue will help.
4. **Vet first.** If your animal seems unwell, call your vet before you post. Members share experience; nobody here diagnoses.
5. **Kind methods only.** No advice to hit, shock, prong or choke-chain an animal.
6. **Keep it private.** No home addresses, live locations or other people's details.
7. **No uninvited DMs.** Ask first. Report anyone who doesn't.
8. **Your photos, your animals.** Share photos you took, of animals you look after.
9. **No ads or spam.** Nobody can pay for recommendations or rank.
10. **Be kind, or be gone.** No shaming, pile-ons or hate. Mods remove posts and people who break the rules.
