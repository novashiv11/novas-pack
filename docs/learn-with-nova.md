# Learn with Nova: Season 1 plan

**10 Oct 2026.** Nine short episodes, one per dog skill in `data/skills.js`. Each clip also plays inside the app on that skill's page.

## Read this first

- **It is "our journey", not expert instruction.** Nova is a young German Shepherd learning with her human, and every dog is different. Say so in every caption: "Our journey, not expert instruction. Stuck? Ask a qualified reward-based trainer or your vet."
- **Every clip is checked by a reward-based trainer before posting.** No check, no post. Keep a note of who checked it and when in `docs/decisions.md`.
- **Reward-based only.** No punishment and no aversive tools (no prong, choke or shock collars, no leash jerks, no shouting) anywhere in frame, in the voiceover or in the captions.
- **Where to film.** Off-lead work happens at home or on private land only. In public, Nova wears her muzzle and a lead of 2 m or less. That is Irish restricted-breed law: German Shepherds (Alsatians) must be muzzled and on a strong lead of no more than 2 m, held by a competent person aged 16 or over, in a public place. A long line is longer than 2 m, so it stays on private land. The rules were under review in 2022, so check the current law before filming outdoors.
- **Muzzle first.** Do not film outdoors in public until the muzzle episode is done and Nova is happy in it. Until then, indoors and garden only, as with the site photos.
- **Privacy.** Never show an exact location, house number, street sign or car plate. Ask anyone who appears for permission first, and keep children out of the shot.
- **Save each clip as `clips/<id>.mp4`.** Muted, under 3 MB, square (1080 x 1080) or 4:5 (1080 x 1350), about 15 to 30 seconds. If a file is over 3 MB, trim it or export at 720 px wide. Burn the on-screen text into the video, since the clip is muted.
- **Voice.** Nova is the "professional human handler". Warm, a little bossy in a loving way, adult. No baby talk.

## Ep 1. Trick: spin (`trick-spin`)

**Level:** Fun · **Length:** 15 s · **Film:** indoors, grippy floor, a few treats.
**On-screen title:** "Teach a spin"

| Shot | Time | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 3 s | Close-up of a treat held at Nova's nose. Title appears. |
| 2 | 3 to 8 s | Hand draws a big circle. Nova turns all the way round, gets the treat. |
| 3 | 8 to 12 s | Same again with a smaller circle, then just a small hand signal. |
| 4 | 12 to 15 s | Wide shot: Nova spins on the word "spin". She is very pleased with herself. |

**On-screen steps**
1. Lure a circle
2. Reward the full turn
3. Make it smaller
4. Add the word "spin"

**Common mistake:** Many fast spins in a row, or spinning on a slippery floor. Keep it short, on a grippy surface.
**When to see a professional:** Your vet, if your dog avoids turning one way, limps or seems stiff. A qualified reward-based trainer, if they get frustrated or stressed.

## Ep 2. Muzzle training (`muzzle`)

**Level:** Next steps · **Length:** 25 s · **Film:** indoors. Use a well-fitted basket muzzle.
**On-screen title:** "Muzzle, the friendly way"

| Shot | Time | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 4 s | Muzzle on the floor with a treat inside. Nova sniffs it. Title appears. |
| 2 | 4 to 10 s | Nova pushes her nose in and eats. A second treat goes through the front. |
| 3 | 10 to 16 s | A hand touches the straps while she keeps eating. |
| 4 | 16 to 21 s | Strap fastened for one second, treat through the front, then off again. |
| 5 | 21 to 25 s | Nova wears it calmly indoors, tail soft. End card: "In Ireland, German Shepherds wear one in public. We make it a good thing." |

**On-screen steps**
1. Treats inside the muzzle
2. Straps, slowly
3. On for one second, then reward
4. Short and easy walks

**Common mistake:** Putting it on before she likes it, or only using it when something unpleasant is coming.
**When to see a professional:** A qualified reward-based trainer, to check the fit and your pace if your dog panics or paws at it. Your vet, for sore skin or any breathing trouble. Never leave a muzzle on an unattended dog.

## Ep 3. Settle on a mat (`settle`)

**Level:** Foundation · **Length:** 20 s · **Film:** indoors, with a folded blanket or mat.
**On-screen title:** "Settle on a mat"

| Shot | Time | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 3 s | Mat on the floor. Nova looks at it. Title appears. |
| 2 | 3 to 8 s | Nova steps on the mat. A treat drops onto it. |
| 3 | 8 to 14 s | She lies down. Treats arrive every few seconds. |
| 4 | 14 to 20 s | Wide shot: Nova relaxed on the mat while her human reads. |

**On-screen steps**
1. Reward paws on the mat
2. Reward lying down
3. Add time slowly
4. Say "settle" as she lies down

**Common mistake:** Asking for a long settle too early. Build it one second at a time.
**When to see a professional:** Your vet, if your dog cannot relax or paces and pants at rest, as pain or worry can be behind it. Then a qualified reward-based trainer or veterinary behaviourist.

## Ep 4. Leave it (`leave-it`)

**Level:** Foundation · **Length:** 20 s · **Film:** indoors, boring treats in one hand and better treats in the other.
**On-screen title:** "Leave it"

| Shot | Time | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 3 s | Close-up of a closed fist with a treat. Nova sniffs it. Title appears. |
| 2 | 3 to 8 s | Nova turns away. "Yes", and a better treat comes from the other hand. |
| 3 | 8 to 14 s | A treat on the floor under a hand. Nova looks up at her human and gets rewarded. |
| 4 | 14 to 20 s | Hand lifts. Nova keeps looking at her human, not the treat. Big reward. |

**On-screen steps**
1. Fist closed, wait for the turn-away
2. Reward from the other hand
3. Treat on the floor, hand over it
4. Uncover it slowly

**Common mistake:** Going to dropped items too fast, or paying with something less exciting than what she left.
**When to see a professional:** Your vet and a qualified reward-based trainer, if your dog guards food or objects, stiffens, growls or snaps. Call your vet straight away if they swallow something unsafe.

## Ep 5. Wait at doors (`wait-doors`)

**Level:** Foundation · **Length:** 20 s · **Film:** at the garden door, Nova on a lead.
**On-screen title:** "Wait at doors"

| Shot | Time | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 3 s | Wide shot: a hand on the door handle, Nova sitting beside. Title appears. |
| 2 | 3 to 8 s | The door opens a little. Nova stays. Treat. |
| 3 | 8 to 13 s | Nova steps forward, so the door calmly closes. No fuss. |
| 4 | 13 to 20 s | Door fully open, Nova waits, "okay", and she walks through. |

**On-screen steps**
1. Door opens a little
2. Stays? Treat
3. Rushes? Door closes
4. Release with "okay"

**Common mistake:** Opening the door wide before she has learned to wait, so rushing through pays off.
**When to see a professional:** Your vet and a qualified reward-based trainer, if your dog bolts through doors in a panic or is afraid to leave the house.

## Ep 6. Loose-lead walking (`loose-lead`)

**Level:** Foundation · **Length:** 25 s · **Film:** the garden first. In public, Nova wears her muzzle and a lead of 2 m or less.
**On-screen title:** "Loose-lead walking"

| Shot | Time | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 4 s | Close-up of Nova's shoulder and a slack loop of lead. Title appears. |
| 2 | 4 to 10 s | A treat at the hip. Nova walks beside her human. |
| 3 | 10 to 16 s | The lead tightens, so her human stops and waits. Nova looks back. |
| 4 | 16 to 22 s | Lead slack. Reward, then walk on. |
| 5 | 22 to 25 s | Wide shot: Nova walks on a loose lead, muzzle on if in public. |

**On-screen steps**
1. Reward beside you
2. Tight lead? Stop
3. Slack lead? Reward and go
4. Treat less often over time

**Common mistake:** Expecting calm walking in a busy place straight away. Build up from easy spots with lots of treats.
**When to see a professional:** Your vet, if the pulling began suddenly. A qualified reward-based trainer, if your dog lunges, barks or freezes around dogs, people or traffic.

## Ep 7. Handling and vet-visit prep (`handling`)

**Level:** Next steps · **Length:** 25 s · **Film:** indoors, with a folded towel for the chin rest.
**On-screen title:** "Vet-visit practice"

| Shot | Time | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 4 s | Nova on the floor. A hand touches her shoulder for one second, then a treat. Title appears. |
| 2 | 4 to 10 s | Gentle touch to a paw, then an ear, then a lip. A treat after each. |
| 3 | 10 to 16 s | Nova walks away. Her human pauses and lets her. |
| 4 | 16 to 21 s | Nova rests her chin on a folded towel: her "ready" signal. |
| 5 | 21 to 25 s | End card: "Next step: pop into the vet clinic just for a treat and a hello, with no exam." |

**On-screen steps**
1. Touch, then treat
2. Paws, ears, mouth
3. She can walk away
4. Chin rest means "ready"

**Common mistake:** Holding on when she tries to leave. Stop at the first sign of discomfort.
**When to see a professional:** Your vet first, if your dog flinches, yelps or avoids touch in one place, since it may be pain. A qualified reward-based trainer, if they growl or snap.

## Ep 8. Calm greetings (`greetings`)

**Level:** Next steps · **Length:** 25 s · **Film:** at home, with one consenting adult. Nova is behind a baby gate or on a lead.
**On-screen title:** "Calm greetings"

| Shot | Time | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 4 s | A friend knocks. Nova waits behind a baby gate. Title appears. |
| 2 | 4 to 10 s | All four paws down, treat. The friend ignores her until she is calm. |
| 3 | 10 to 17 s | Nova does a sit or a hand touch, gets a treat, then the friend says hello. |
| 4 | 17 to 22 s | Nova steps away. Her human lets her, and the friend does not follow. |
| 5 | 22 to 25 s | Scatter of treats on the floor to end the hello. |

**On-screen steps**
1. Four paws down, treat
2. Sit or hand touch, then hello
3. Let her choose
4. Keep hellos short

**Common mistake:** Letting a few jumps slide because a guest says they don't mind. Mixed rules confuse dogs.
**When to see a professional:** Your vet and a qualified reward-based trainer, if your dog growls, snaps or is afraid of visitors or other dogs. Do not push greetings.

## Ep 9. Come when called (`recall`)

**Level:** Foundation · **Length:** 25 s · **Film:** indoors, the garden or private land only. No long line in public.
**On-screen title:** "Come when called"

| Shot | Time | What we see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 4 s | Her human crouches at the far end of the garden. Nova is mid-sniff. Title appears. |
| 2 | 4 to 9 s | One happy "Nova, come!" and she sprints over. |
| 3 | 9 to 15 s | Close-up: three treats in a row as she arrives. |
| 4 | 15 to 21 s | Repeat from further away, a little more distance each time. |
| 5 | 21 to 25 s | Her human releases her back to play. She is thrilled. |

**On-screen steps**
1. Call once, happy voice
2. Three treats in a row
3. Add distance slowly
4. Then back to play

**Common mistake:** Calling your dog and then ending the fun or doing something they dislike. That teaches them to ignore you.
**When to see a professional:** A qualified reward-based trainer, if your dog bolts from fear or ignores you around other animals despite steady practice. Your vet, if it is a sudden change.
