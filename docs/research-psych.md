# Research: the psychology behind "Level up together"

Legend: `*` = verified by search; `†` = from memory.

## 1. Behaviour change

- **Self-determination theory:** motivation lasts with autonomy, competence, relatedness [1†]. *In the app:* EXISTS (skill levels). ADD "Swap this nudge" (1 of 3). Relatedness: Nova and the opt-in share card, no chat.
- **Habit time:** median 66 days to automaticity (18 to 254); small sample; a missed day did no harm [2*]. *In the app:* EXISTS ("3 days wins the week"). CHANGE: show rolling "11 of last 14 days", not consecutive days.
- **Implementation intentions:** if-then plans, d = 0.65 on goal attainment [3*]. *In the app:* ADD an onboarding cue picker ("when I clip the lead"); the card reads "When ___, I'll ___".
- **Identity:** noun framing ("be a voter") beat verb framing [4†]. *In the app:* PARTLY (ranks). CHANGE copy: "You showed up. Handler." Ranks never drop.
- **Tiny habits (Fogg):** shrink it, celebrate at once [5†]; practitioner model, not RCT-proven. *In the app:* EXISTS (1-minute wins). ADD "Make it smaller" (10 seconds); after a gap, the only ask.
- **Goal gradient:** effort rises near a reward [6†]. *In the app:* ADD real progress only ("1 session to Level 2"); no pre-filled bars.
- **Fresh start:** effort rises after Mondays, month starts, birthdays [7*]. *In the app:* ADD a Monday "clean lead" card, restart on any day, the dog's gotcha day.
- **Self-compassion and procrastination:** links to more health behaviours, r = .25 [8†], and motivation after failure [9†]. *In the app:* EXISTS (guilt-free tone). CHANGE: welcome-back never mentions missed days.
- **Temporal motivation:** motivation falls with delay [10†]. *In the app:* reward within 2 seconds of "done"; cue at moments the dog is already keen (meals, walks).
- **Overjustification:** expected, engagement- or completion-contingent tangible rewards cut intrinsic motivation, d = -0.28 to -0.40; positive feedback helped [11*]. *In the app:* EXISTS (XP derived, capped). CHANGE: milestones state competence ("Maya now waits 5 seconds"); no prizes; ADD "XP off" toggle.
- **Variable rewards, streaks:** variable-ratio schedules persist most [12†]; streaks exploit loss aversion [13†]. *In the app:* EXISTS (no streak guilt). Keep: no mystery rewards, paid freezes, "streak lost" screens.
- **Gamification meta-analysis:** small effects: cognitive g = .49, motivational .36, behavioural .25 [14*]. *In the app:* keep points light; lean on Nova's story and competence feedback.
- **Social accountability:** progress monitoring helps, more when reported to others [15†]. *In the app:* ADD an optional weekly buddy note via the share sheet; no public boards.

## 2. Pictures for instructions

Pictures help recall when tied to text and aid comprehension when they show spatial relationships, most for low-literacy readers; applied results are mixed, so test [16*]. Dual coding [17†] and multimedia principles [18†] explain why; decorative pictures don't help [19†]. Rules:

1. One action per picture; caption of 8 words or fewer, starting with a verb.
2. Show the result: a before/after pair (standing, then sitting) at the reward moment.
3. One arrow, one colour, for the hand or treat path; number steps 1-2-3.
4. Flat and minimal: Nova SVG plus one hand, no background, high contrast, alt text.
5. Test: show 3 people the picture alone; if they can't name the action, redraw.

## 3. Starting-level quiz

- Ask about the last 2 weeks, with counts and places, not "how good is your dog". I found no direct study of owner-report accuracy for obedience; the C-BARQ was validated against consultants in a referred sample [20*]. Treat answers as a first guess.
- Normalise, say "if torn, pick the lower one", give no XP or badges for placement, offer a no-penalty step down after two misses. Six questions, about a minute. Test recall only in a fenced area or on a long line.

Options map to levels 0 to 4 in order.

1. **Recall.** Called away from something interesting, my dog: ignores me or untried / comes indoors only / comes in a quiet garden, 7 of 10 / comes on a long line in a park, most times / comes in a safe off-lead area past distractions, 9 of 10.
2. **Loose lead.** On walks the lead is: tight from the first step / loose at home, tight outside / loose for half a quiet street / loose on a quiet street, tight at something exciting / loose past other dogs or people, most times.
3. **Settle.** On a mat or bed my dog: won't stay down / settles under a minute / settles 5+ minutes at home / settles 10+ minutes with me moving about or visitors in / settles 15+ minutes somewhere new (café).
4. **Leave it.** With food in my hand or on the floor my dog: grabs it / waits a second when my hand covers a treat / ignores a treat under my hand for 3 seconds / walks past dropped food on lead when asked, 7 of 10 / leaves dropped food or wildlife outdoors, 9 of 10.
5. **Wait at doors.** At doors, gates and the car my dog: barges through / pauses a second at the front door / waits for my "ok" at the front door / waits even when excited (visitor, walk time) / waits with a distraction (doorbell, dog outside), 9 of 10.
6. **Handling.** When I touch paws, ears and mouth my dog: pulls away, tenses or growls (vet first, pain is common) / accepts body stroking, avoids paws, ears, mouth / lets me touch paws and ears for 3 seconds, relaxed / lets me lift a lip, check ears, hold a paw 5+ seconds / stays relaxed through a full mock check-up, also with family.

## 4. The human-animal bond

- **Well supported:** dog owners tend to be more physically active (observational) [21†]; people often report comfort, routine and connection from pets (mainly qualitative) [22*].
- **Mixed:** mental health. A 54-study review found 17 positive, 19 mixed, 13 no effect, 5 negative [23*]. Attachment quality matters more than ownership; data are mostly cross-sectional [24*].
- **Overclaimed:** "cures" anxiety or depression, lowers blood pressure or cortisol, extends life, or that people without a pet are worse off.
- **Safe wording:** "A walk with a dog is an easy excuse to get outside." "For many people a dog brings comfort, routine and company." "Designed to make small habits easier; we haven't tested that it improves wellbeing." "No pet yet? You're still in the pack."

## 6. Top 10 changes (impact/effort) and red lines

1. If-then cue picker; card reads "When ___, I'll ___" (high/low).
2. "Make it smaller" on every card; after 2+ missed days show only that (high/low).
3. Rolling "x of last 14 days", welcome-back without missed-day count, Monday fresh-start card (high/low).
4. Celebration within 2 seconds of "done": Nova line plus sticker (high/low).
5. Competence-language milestones plus "XP off" toggle (medium/low).
6. Swap-the-nudge; opt-in reminder at the user's cue time, max 1 a day (medium/low).
7. Six-question placement quiz with step-down (medium/medium).
8. Picture-first step cards, top 3 skills first (medium-high/high).
9. Gotcha-day landmark and 30-day journal look-back (medium/low).
10. Optional weekly buddy note via share sheet (low-medium/low).

**Red lines:**
- No streak-loss or guilt messages, paid freezes, mystery rewards, rank loss.
- No XP for opens, shares, likes, the quiz or journal length.
- No fake or pre-filled progress. Label the "1% a day" chart an illustration; learning curves flatten.
- No health claims, aversive methods, or off-lead tests near roads. Vet first.
- No default leaderboards or chat; reminders opt-in.
