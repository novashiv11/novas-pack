# Launch kit: "Level up together" (the app)

For Shiv. Copy and paste; film everything with Nova at home in about 15 minutes per video. One video goes to four places: Instagram Reels (main), TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels.

**Link:** novashiv11.github.io/novas-pack

**Before you post anything:** use the app with Nova yourself for a week. Every screen you show must be your real data. Never show the "SAMPLE" pet as if it were Nova.

---

## 1. Bio (Instagram, under 150 characters)

> Professional human handler. Still waiting on that raise.
> Level up with your dog, 1% a day: free app 👇

Put the link in the bio's link field. In Stories, use the link sticker.

## 2. Saved reply and auto-reply for "APP"

Use this when someone comments or DMs "APP".

> Here you go 🐾 novashiv11.github.io/novas-pack
> Open it in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), then tap Install (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen).
> Free, no account, everything stays on your phone. Tell me your dog's first skill level-up. I'll pretend to be surprised.

## 3. Launch week: 5 Reels

Each Reel is 15–25 seconds. Show the hook as on-screen text in the first 2 seconds. End on the app screen and "Link in bio".

**Rule for outdoor shots:** Nova is always muzzled and on a lead of 2 m or less, held by someone over 16 (Irish law for restricted breeds). Showing it calmly is part of the brand.

### Day 1 (Mon): "My dog has a level now."

- **Shots:**
  1. Nova doing one thing well (sit or settle).
  2. Phone screen: Nova's partner card (level and stats).
  3. Nova's face.
- **On-screen text:** "My dog has a level now." → "Recall: level 1. We're working on it." → "Free app. Link in bio."
- **Caption:**
  > Small daily training sessions, real-life adventures, and your dog levels up. So do you. 1% a day.
  > Free, no account, private on your phone. Comment APP for the link.
  > #NewToThePack #NovasPack #dogtraining

### Day 2 (Tue): "1% better today."

- **Shots:**
  1. A 5-minute training session, sped up.
  2. You ticking the "1% today" card.
  3. Nova asleep afterwards.
- **On-screen text:** "5 minutes of recall." → "5 slow breaths for me." → "1% better. That's it. That's the post."
- **Caption:**
  > One tiny step for your dog, one for you. No streaks to lose, no guilt. Comment APP.

### Day 3 (Wed, Ask the Pack): "What should Nova learn next?"

- **Shots:** the skill list in the app, scrolled slowly, with Nova looking at the camera.
- **On-screen text:** "11 dog skills. I've mastered… lying down. Pick my next one 👇"
- **Caption:**
  > Recall, loose lead, settle, leave it… What should I level up this week? Most-asked one becomes Monday's challenge.

  This is a real question. Use the answers.

### Day 5 (Fri): "The Adventure Book."

- **Shots:**
  1. Nova (muzzled, on lead) on a new surface, bridge or rain walk.
  2. You tapping to earn the stamp in the app.
- **On-screen text:** "First rain walk. Stamp earned." → "About 30 real-life stamps to collect."
- **Caption:**
  > Not screen time. Outside time, with a souvenir. What's your dog's next first?

### Day 7 (Sun, #PackWalk): "One week in."

- **Shots:**
  1. Nova's real partner card after the week (use the in-app Share button).
  2. Your Growth Journal week view. Show only what you're happy to share.
- **On-screen text:** "Week 1: 9 sessions, 3 walks, 1 adventure." Use your real numbers.
- **Caption:**
  > Not perfect. Better. Your turn: comment APP and share your partner card next Sunday with #PackWalk.

## 4. Stories (every launch day, 3 frames)

1. A photo of Nova with a poll: "Did you train your dog today? Yes / Tomorrow 😅". A poll is fine here: it's a question, not a claim.
2. A screenshot of the app screen from that day's Reel.
3. The link sticker "Free app 🐾" with "Install: Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone)".

## 5. Ongoing (fits the 5-hour week)

- **Mon:** the weekly challenge is one skill (from Wednesday's answers). "Log 3 sessions this week."
- **Wed:** Ask the Pack, a training question. Answer with a reward-based tip plus "if you're worried, see a qualified trainer or your vet".
- **Sat:** Shout-out Saturday features members' partner cards. Ask consent each time (template in `global-plan.md`, Appendix C).
- **Sun:** #PackWalk, a real walk, ending with "stamp earned".
- **Learn with Nova clips** (`docs/learn-with-nova.md`) double as Reels **and** go into the app (`clips/<skill-id>.mp4`).

## 6. Never say

- User numbers we don't have ("thousands of dogs are levelling up"). We have no analytics, by design, so we don't know.
- "Certified", "qualified" or "professional trainer" about Shiv, until it's true. Trainer ranks in the app are learning milestones.
- Health promises ("fixes anxiety", "heals"). Vet first, always.
- "Pokémon" or other game brands. Say "a game where your dog levels up".
- Anything about selling or rehoming animals.

## 7. How to tell it's working (one number a week)

There's no tracking in the app, on purpose. Use Instagram's own **link taps** (Professional dashboard → the post's insights) and the number of "APP" comments and DMs. Write them in a note every Sunday. If a Reel gets more APP comments than the others, make two more like it.
