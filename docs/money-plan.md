# Money plan: how Nova's Pack earns, on autopilot

**Version 1 · 9 Oct 2026 · Status: proposal. Prices and splits are Shiv's call.**

## The answer in 5 lines

1. **Money comes in this order:** trainer workshops and walk sponsors, then Nova's brand posts and merch drops, then verified listings once there are 200+ local members. Plus tier much later. Never: pay-to-rank, selling data, pet-sale ads.
2. **Realistic year one: about €500–2,500** (estimate). This is a side income that pays for itself, not a salary yet.
3. **About 90% of the money admin can run itself:** sign-ups, payments, receipts, reminders, waitlists, merch printing, shipping and VAT. The stack is free or close to free until money comes in.
4. **The rest stays human on purpose:** approving members, moderating, running walks, asking consent for Shout-outs, vetting sponsors and listings. Automating those would break the honesty and safety rules, and they're what members trust.
5. **Money admin: about 30 minutes a week**, plus one tax return a year.

## Tax and paperwork (keep it light)

- **Register the business name** "Nova's Pack" with the CRO before taking money, because you're trading under a name that isn't your own: form RBN1 on core.cro.ie, about €20 online ([Xero guide](https://www.xero.com/ie/guides/how-to-register-a-business-in-ireland/)).
- **Income tax:** you're a PAYE employee. If your side income is **under €5,000 a year**, declare it on your yearly Income Tax Return in Revenue **myAccount**: no self-assessment needed ([Revenue](https://www.revenue.ie/en/additional-incomes/is-your-extra-income-taxable/index.aspx), [Citizens Information](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/money-and-tax/tax/income-tax/tax-return-non-paye-income/)). Above that, register for self-assessment (Form TR1).
- **VAT:** no registration needed below **€42,500** (services) or **€85,000** (goods) a year. Fourthwall handles merch VAT as seller of record ([Xero VAT guide](https://www.xero.com/ie/guides/vat-threshold-ireland/)).
- **Insurance:** trainer's own policy (named on group walks) plus public liability for you as organiser before the first walk.
- **Ads law:** **#Ad** on every sponsored or paid post, at the start ([CCPC/ASAI](https://www.ccpc.ie/news-and-media/news/article/2023/10/26/if-in-doubt--label-it!---new-guidance-on-social-media-advertising-for-influencers-issued-by-ccpc-and-asai)).
- **Before the first euro:**
  - Check your employment contract for any limits on outside work.
  - The CRO business-name register is public and the form asks for your address. If you'd rather not publish your home address, ask an accountant about options (e.g. a registered business address).
  - Once the site earns money (sponsors, listings), the law expects it to show who runs it: a name, a geographic address and an email (S.I. 68/2003). The privacy notice will need your full name then too.
  - Marketing messages (e.g. "new merch drop!") only go to people who've opted in. Announcements about the pack itself are fine.
- Not legal or tax advice. A one-off chat with an accountant (often free for a first call) is worth it once revenue passes €1,000.
