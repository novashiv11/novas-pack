# Launch kit

**What this is:** everything to post in launch week, in Nova's voice. Copy-paste ready. Text in `[square brackets]` is for Shiv to fill in.

**Status:** draft v1, 9 Oct 2026. Being reviewed.

**Before you post anything:**
- [ ] WhatsApp Community is set up (`docs/community-kit.md`, section 1).
- [ ] PACK quick reply saved (`docs/community-kit.md`, section 9).
- [ ] Website is live: https://novashiv11.github.io/novas-pack/
- [ ] Link in bio updated.

**Honesty rules for every post:** no invented numbers, no "join 100s of members", no fake polls. Mention the Pack Walk, map and Learn with Nova only as "coming soon" until they're running.

**Filming rule:** German Shepherds are a restricted breed in Ireland. In any public place, film Nova muzzled and on a lead no longer than 2 m. Off-lead and muzzle-free shots: at home, in the garden, or in a hired secure field.

## 2. Five Reel scripts

  > DM me PACK to join. Ages 16+.

  > #NovasPack #NewToThePack #LimerickPets #Limerick #GermanShepherd

## 6. Shout-out Saturday

### Rules

1. You must be a pack member. Any species counts. No pet yet? You can still be "most helpful".
2. Each week there are up to **3 picks**:
   - **Newcomer:** someone who joined in the last 2 weeks.
   - **Most helpful:** someone who answered questions or helped another member that week.
   - **Challenge favourite:** Nova's favourite entry to the week's challenge.
3. Each pet is featured **at most once every 8 weeks**, so everyone gets a turn.
4. Real photos of your own animals only.
5. **We always ask first.** Picks get a message by Friday evening; no reply or "no thanks" means we pick someone else. You can opt out for good any time.
6. Never featured: animals for sale or rehoming, photos showing a home address or exact location, or anyone under 16.

Keep a simple list: date, member, pet, category. Check it before choosing so nobody's featured twice in 8 weeks.

### Friday "can we feature you?" message

> Hi [name]! Nova would love to feature [pet name] in tomorrow's Shout-out Saturday as our [newcomer / most helpful member / #Challenge favourite] 🐾 Can we use [this photo] and tag @[handle]? Totally fine to say no. Just reply yes or no by [9am Saturday].

### Caption template

> **Shout-out Saturday** 🐾 Meet some of the pack:
>
> 🆕 **Newcomer:** welcome, [pet name] the [animal], and @[handle]!
> 🤝 **Most helpful:** @[handle], thank you for [what they did].
> 🏆 **Challenge favourite:** [pet name] for #[Challenge]. [One witty line from Nova.]
>
> Everyone gets a turn. Want in? DM me PACK. Ages 16+.
> #ShoutOutSaturday #NovasPack
