**Version 2 · 9 Oct 2026.** Reviewed by growth, brand and legal reviewers.

## Ground rules for every post

- **Honest:** no invented numbers, no "join hundreds of members", no fake polls. The Pack Walk, map and Learn with Nova are "coming soon" until they run.
- **Muzzle on in public:** German Shepherds are a restricted breed in Ireland. In any public place, film Nova muzzled and on a lead of 2 m or less. Off-lead or muzzle-free shots only at home, in a garden, or in a hired secure field.
- **Ages:** the chat is 18+. Instagram challenges and Shout-out Saturday are 16+.
- **Paid or gifted?** Put **#Ad** at the start of the caption ([CCPC/ASAI](https://www.ccpc.ie/news-and-media/news/article/2023/10/26/if-in-doubt--label-it!---new-guidance-on-social-media-advertising-for-influencers-issued-by-ccpc-and-asai)).

## 3. Five Reels

**For every Reel:**
- 7–20 seconds, with the hook on screen in the first second.
- Captions on every line (most people watch muted).
- One call to action ("DM me PACK"). Tag the location as Limerick.
- **Max 5 hashtags.** Hashtags don't add reach, so tagging @novathehumanhandler matters more.
- End with a share prompt: shares by DM are a strong signal.
- Try new hooks as **Trial Reels** first. They're shown to non-followers only, so a dud costs nothing.
- Nova's own sounds (snores, sighs, zoomies) beat trending audio.

## 7. Shout-out Saturday

### Rules

1. You must be a pack member, aged 16+. Any species. No pet yet? You can still be "most helpful".
2. Up to **3 picks** a week:
   - **Newcomer:** the next person in joining order who hasn't been featured.
   - **Most helpful:** nominated by members ("Who helped you this week?" in Thursday's chat).
   - **Challenge favourite:** picked by Nova's human from entries on Instagram **or** in the chat. Never chosen by likes.
3. Each pet is featured **at most once every 8 weeks**; we rotate so lots of pets get a turn.
4. Only photos the member took or may share.
5. **Always ask first.** Ask by Friday evening; no reply or "no thanks" means we pick someone else. Screenshot each "yes" with the date.
6. Never featured: animals for sale or rehoming, anything showing a home address or exact location, anyone under 16.
7. Members can ask for a feature to be taken down at any time.

**Rotation log:** date · member · pet · category · consent screenshot ✔. Check it before choosing.

### Friday consent message

> Hi [name]! Nova would love to feature [pet name] in tomorrow's Shout-out Saturday as our [newcomer / most helpful member / #Challenge favourite] 🐾 Can we use [this photo] and tag @[handle]? It's totally fine to say no. Just reply yes or no by [9am Saturday].
